Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Hi Kit Ltd ("KIT", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, sign up for early access or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal. When you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Early access information: Your email address when you voluntarily submit it to join our early access list.
  • Contact form information: Your name, email address and message when you contact us through the website.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system collected automatically via standard server logs for security and performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To notify you about updates, launch details, and early access to KIT.
  • To respond to your inquiries or support requests.
  • To maintain website security and improve overall performance.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform (such as email service providers, Google, which stores our early access list, and Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our inbox) under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion or update of your personal information.
  • The right to unsubscribe from marketing and early access communications at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us using the contact form. Hi Kit Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales.
